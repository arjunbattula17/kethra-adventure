# Texture Audit

Every texture the player sees in the first hour (the opening cinematic, the ship interior, the
galaxy reveal and Kethra), with what it costs and whether it is sized to how it is actually seen.

- **Generated by** `tools/texture-audit.mjs` (per-scene inventory + measured texel density) and
  `tools/texture-audit-md.mjs` (this table). Re-run both after any texture change; the JSON reports
  land in `reports/`.
- **Originals:** every file changed in this pass is in git at revision `6050a6a`. Restore any
  single texture with `git checkout 6050a6a -- <path>`. Procedural textures are code; their
  history is the git history of the builder named in the table.

## How it was measured
- **Memory** is the GPU footprint three.js allocates: width × height × 4 bytes (all textures
  upload as RGBA8 in WebGL, whatever the file format), × 4/3 for the mip chain. Textures that share
  one image (clones) are counted once, because three.js uploads a shared image once.
- **Texel density** is measured, not guessed: for every mesh that uses a texture, the tool sums
  world-space triangle area and UV area (including the material's repeat), then reports
  `sqrt(uvArea × texW × texH / worldArea)` in texels per metre. The table shows the minimum
  (the most stretched use, which sets the resolution a texture needs) and the median.
- **On-screen size.** Interior camera: 62° vertical FOV, so a 1080p frame shows about 900 ÷ d
  pixels per metre at distance d (450 px/m at 2 m). Kethra: 65° FOV. Cinematic backgrounds (sky,
  planets) are judged by their measured on-screen footprint instead: planets peak at a 20–70 px
  radius during the reveal, the sun at 84 px.

## The standard
A texture is **right-sized** when its least-dense use lands between 0.5× and 2× of its tier's
target. Below that it looks muddy; above it, it wastes memory.

| Tier | Target texels/m | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| hero | 1024 | readable screens, labels, stencils, close-up interactive props |
| interior | 512 | walkable ship interior, surfaces at 1–3 m |
| ground | 512 | the ground under the player's feet (viewed at 1.7 m) |
| exterior | 256 | outdoor props and plants at 2–6 m |
| footprint | measured px | sky and planets: sized to their on-screen footprint |
| soft | — | AO, contact shadows, glows, grime and dirt overlays: soft by design |

**The one justified increase:** the ship's wall panels were measured at 109–134 texels/m, a
quarter of the interior standard, on the largest surfaces of the first scene the player walks
around in. They are now painted at 2× in the same design space (same seams, bolts and layout,
normal strength compensated). That costs +25 MB, which the duplicate sharing more than pays for.

**Format:** all shipped textures are JPEG (colour, roughness, normals) or PNG (anything with
alpha), mipmapped, and power-of-two after this pass (the two non-power-of-two files, the decal
sheet at 2000² and the flower atlas at 1008×981, were resampled). GPU texture compression
(KTX2/Basis) is not used yet. In a browser that means adding a build step and a bundled
transcoder. It is logged as a backlog item, with an expected further ~4–6× cut in GPU memory for
colour maps [unsourced — estimate].

## Visual verification
Before/after renders of fixed camera views (engine paused, one frame rendered by hand) with
`tools/capture-views.mjs`, diffed with `tools/compare-renders.mjs`:
- **Ship, duplicate sharing:** mean difference 0.58/255 against a run-to-run noise floor of
  0.55–0.66. Pixel-equivalent.
- **Ship, after right-sizing:** 0.56/255. Within noise.
- **Kethra, after right-sizing:** 1.1–2.1/255 on most views against a 0.39 noise floor. The
  residual is finer bark and leaf detail resolving differently at the new mip levels, not a
  colour change. Mean-colour drift per file is 0.00–1.07 levels (normal maps excepted; their
  stored length changes on re-normalization, but three.js normalizes in the shader, so lighting
  direction is unchanged).
- **Two regressions caught and fixed on the way,** both now guarded in `tools/resize-textures.mjs`:
  (1) a canvas-based resize turned transparent leaf-atlas texels black, darkening every canopy;
  (2) a normal-map name match caught `Bark_NormalTree.jpg` (a colour map) and re-normalized it
  into saturated orange.

## Remaining issues (not fixed in this pass)
- **Grove rocks are under-resolved** (`Rocks_Diffuse`, 85–104 texels/m). It is a painted island
  atlas, not a tiling texture, so raising repeat would break it. The fix is new UVs or a tiling
  detail map. Backlog.
- **Style outlier:** the Kethra ground and ruin pieces use `lichen_rock`, a photo-scanned Poly
  Haven material, beside Quaternius's hand-painted stylized plants and rocks. It reads as two art
  styles in one frame. Replacing it changes the look, so it is a team decision (DECISIONS.md).
- **The sky is soft by resolution:** at 11 px/degree against the 23 px/degree a 1080p frame
  shows. The point-sprite starfields carry the sharp stars. A cubemap bake would allow more
  resolution without the equirect's waste; not worth it yet.
- **Unused files in the build:** `public/models/quaternius-nature/glTF/*.png` (20 files, 38 MB)
  are copies of the textures in `Textures/`, which is where the kit loader actually fetches from.
  They are never requested but ship in every deploy (the build is 108 MB). Proposed for removal in
  DECISIONS.md; not deleted, since deleting assets needs the team's sign-off.
- **Hero canvases under 1024:** a few stencil and label canvases measure 300–600 texels/m against
  the hero target. All are legible in play; they are marked in the table for a later pass.
