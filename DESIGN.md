# Design

A description of the finished game, built on what exists. Items marked *(proposed)* depend on a
team decision in DECISIONS.md. Story facts come from LORE.md; the look from ART_BIBLE.md.

## Hook
*A survey ship goes dark past the edge of the charts. You wake it up, and to get home you have
to read a dead civilization's light.*

## Core mechanic and why it's fun
You solve problems by **reading light**: scans, spectra, glowing script, a ritual's colours,
and a signal that pulls ships out of the sky. Your character's stats decide *which* readings you
can make and *which* solutions open. The fun is the moment of insight: the world already
showed you the answer, and your skills let you see it.
- *(proposed, D-2)* The verb is **tune**: the Wren's Deep Scanner reads and emits light
  frequencies.
- *(proposed, D-3)* Every critical-path puzzle has a way through that uses a stat.

## Theme connection (TSA: deep-space RPG, puzzles not combat)
- **Deep space:** a stranded ship in an uncharted system, four worlds, a signal from an ocean
  moon.
- **RPG:** six stats (insight, archaeology, engineering, traversal, persuasion, perception),
  XP and levels with skill points, and stat-gated branching dialogue. Stats are how puzzles and
  conversations open, not a side menu.
- **Puzzles instead of combat:** nobody fights. Danger is darkness, a failing ship, a guardian
  you have to be quiet around, and distance.

## Levels (introduce → develop → twist)

### Level 1 — The Wren *(exists)*
- **Place:** the ship interior after the white sky.
- **Teaches:** movement, interaction, the character sheet, the console (tutorial, first minute).
- **Set piece:** the galaxy reveal. The viewport shows a system no chart contains.
- **Puzzles:**
  - Critical path: the course plot to Kethra *(to be rebuilt as a chart mechanic, D-7)*.
  - Optional harder: route power to a second system *(proposed, D-8)*.
  - Discovery: the corrupted travel logs and the Ship's Library entries.
- **Signature verb:** *introduce* tuning by calibrating the scan to pick Kethra out of the noise.

### Level 2 — Kethra *(exists)*
- **Place:** terraced Kindling ruins under a dimming bioluminescent canopy.
- **People:** Warden Corvenna (trust earned by persuasion or by fixing the valve) and Fen
  Larkspur.
- **Set piece:** the Cistern Heart waking and light flooding back up the terraces.
- **Puzzles:**
  - Critical path: read three scattered inscriptions and call the Heart's colours in the
    true order, which is *not* the order the Aiveth perform.
  - Optional harder: earn Corvenna's deep trust (persuasion 4).
  - Discovery: the last Kindling carving, which matches the Wren's own disaster.
- **Signature verb:** *develop* tuning, calling three colours in sequence.
- **Accessibility:** the colour puzzle gets a second channel (each colour also has a distinct
  glyph shape; BACKLOG B-11).

### Level 3 — Vessek Anchorage *(proposed, D-4)*
- **Place:** a ring of stranded ships lashed to a Kindling ring-station, home to about 300
  people.
- **People:** Harbormaster Ilse Varro, who wants the Wren's hyperdrive core.
- **Set piece:** a white sky hits the ring mid-level. Lights die deck by deck; the player
  restores power in the right order before the hydroponics bay freezes (tension, never harm).
- **Puzzles:**
  - Critical path: bargain for conduit alloy (persuasion or engineering routes).
  - Optional harder: find the Lantern Bay's original log.
  - Discovery: the ledger of every ship pulled in.
- **Signature verb:** *twist*, counter-tuning a white sky.
- **Story reversal (from LORE.md):** the Cistern Hearts are relays for the Choir. Waking
  Kethra's Heart made the signal stronger.

### Later levels *(after level 3 is polished)*
- **Orrun's Reach:** traversal and engineering; a buried relay the size of a town.
- **Isilthe:** the finale; teach the Choir a new song.
- **Ending:** the Wren flies home, and the Choir stops calling ships.

## Structure and flow
- Title → intro (24 s, skippable) → level 1 → reveal → level 2 → level 3.
- The first three levels are the strongest.
- A competent player reaches level 3 in under 10 minutes.
- Progress saves automatically; a level select unlocks as levels are reached *(D-6)*.

## Companion voice
The Wren's ship computer, **ORION** (named from the Orion Interfaces consoles already on screen):
short reactive lines, 12 words at most, spoken through the existing caption and toast channels.
Fen is the companion voice on Kethra.
