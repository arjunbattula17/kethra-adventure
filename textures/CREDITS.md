`space/starfield.jpg` is generated by `tools/prep-starfield.mjs` (procedural, no external
source; deterministic seed, so re-running the tool reproduces it). It replaced NASA SVS's
"An Elsewhere Starfield", whose only repo-sized download (1024x512) was too JPEG-compressed
to survive being the whole sky.

Everything in `planets/` is derived from **[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/)
textures** (licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), themselves based on
NASA elevation and imagery data): `2k_earth_clouds`, `2k_venus_surface`, `2k_mars`, `2k_jupiter`,
`2k_neptune`, `2k_sun`, and `2k_saturn_ring_alpha`, re-mapped into this game's fictional planet
palettes by `tools/prep-planet-textures.mjs` (per-planet luminance ramps that keep the sources'
geological/atmospheric structure while replacing their colour signatures).
