# Storyboard check

The final flow, scene by scene and screen by screen, so the hand-drawn storyboard can be redrawn
to match the game. Each numbered panel is one thing the player sees. Levels 4–5 are not in the
game; don't draw them as playable.

## Title
1. **Title screen.** "KETHRA" in large type, cut by an amber scan line: ink above, sea-green below.
   Kethra turns slowly at the right. Buttons: Continue (only if saved), New game, Controls,
   Settings. No footer; the credits roll at the end of the game.
2. **Controls / Settings** open as panels over the title.

## Intro: "Cold Start" (24 s, skippable with Space, Enter or a click)
3. **Loading screen** with a progress bar and a line of lore.
4. **The Wren drifting** in the dark, letterboxed. The first line appears:
   "Day 23 past the Kessic Drift, hunting lost ships."
5. **The white sky.** The screen floods white and the ship goes dark: "Then the sky went white.
   The Wren went dark."
6. **The reboot.** Lights come back section by section: "Emergency power only…"
7. **The goal** in amber: "Get the Wren flying. Find the way home."

## Level 1: the Wren
8. **Cold open** on the ship deck, letterboxed, with three captions: "Emergency reboot complete.
   Life support: online." / "Navigation, hyperdrive and long-range scan are still dark." / "You are
   the only one aboard awake to do anything about it."
9. **Tutorial step 1, "Take the Helm":** the card tells you to click, then look around with the
   mouse or arrow keys.
10. **Step 2, "Find Your Footing":** walk with W A S D, run with Shift, jump with Space.
11. **Step 3, "Know Your Skills":** Tab opens the character sheet.
12. **Step 4, "Reach the Console":** a waypoint marks the navigation console.
13. **Step 5, "Bring It Online":** press E at the console. The player sits down in the pilot chair.
14. **Caption:** "Navigation online. Reserve power routed to long-range scan."
15. **Galaxy reveal:** the whole system appears around its sun, with the captions "You are stranded,
    alone, in a galaxy no chart has ever mapped." and "Somewhere out there is the truth — and a
    way home."
16. **Course plot** panel over the console: three questions (How far is the transfer? How many days
    at cruise? How many reserve cells?), then "Burn committed."
17. **Ship hub.** HUD: objective top left, level and keys bottom right. Toasts: travel logs
    restored, repair interface online, Kethra in range.
18. **Navigation map:** the solar chart plus a dossier. Kethra is selected; press Set course.
19. **Scan-line transition**, then the loading bar.

## Level 2: Kethra
20. **Chapter card:** "Level 2 · Kethra. Terraced ruins under a dimming canopy. Someone lives here."
21. **The grove plaza** at night: sea-green canopy, Warden Corvenna (dim blue veins, a staff) and
    Fen (bright green).
22. **Dialogue** with the Warden, in a panel in the lower third. Some options are locked behind
    stats (e.g. "persuasion 2").
23. **Inscriptions 1–3** on the terraces. Each opens as a document and is recorded in the journal.
24. **The valve** (optional; earns the Warden's thanks) and **the shrine stele**.
25. **The Wickmoth** circling the chamber approach. Stained-glass wings; the guardian keeps you from
    the Heart.
26. **Close the lantern bloom:** the petals fold, and the Wickmoth settles off the path.
27. **The call-stone:** the Rite panel, with glyph buttons for azure, amber and verdant (plus two
    decoys) and a progress row.
28. **The Heart wakes:** the vanes light one after another, water glows, and the camera shakes
    gently. Card: "Level 2 complete. The Heart wakes."
29. **The last Kindling carving** (optional): a white sky, in carved stone, like the one that hit
    the Wren.
30. **Return pad:** "Return to Ship".

## Back on the Wren
31. **Repair station:** navigation and the Deep Scanner repaired with resonant crystal.
32. **Toast:** "Deep Scanner online. A ring of ships resolves at 95 Mkm." The objective points at
    Vessek.
33. **Map:** Vessek is preselected. Set course.

## Level 3: Vessek Anchorage
34. **Chapter card:** "Level 3 · Vessek Anchorage. Twenty-one stranded ships and one very old ring."
35. **The docking collar:** crates, floor lights, and the Wren's airlock sign "WREN-01".
36. **Dace**, with a headlamp: "You're the amber lights on our east clamp!"
37. **The Lantern Bay concourse:** patchwork lamps, the ring and the planet Vessek through the east
    windows.
38. **Harbormaster Varro** at her desk made of crates. She wants the Wren's hyperdrive core;
    stat-gated offers are shown.
39. **The rehearsal pulse.** The windows flood white and the camera shakes. The lights die deck by
    deck (grow lights, dock, hall, gallery) until only amber emergency strips are left. Caption:
    "Varro: 'Rehearsal pulse. Two years early.'"
40. **HUD:** the frost meter under the objective ("Hydroponics 11.9 °C").
41. **(Optional) Dace's duct:** traversal 2 opens the warm-water valve for more time.
42. **Breaker gallery panel:** six breakers with notes from six crews, a 6-unit bus meter and the
    frost bar.
    - Fail beat: "Overload…" or "The heaters trip… pumps first."
    - Frost-out: "backup warmers bought one more try."
43. **Power restored:** the ring relights in reverse, and the ships outside get their lights back.
44. **The ledger** on the lectern: "Eleven days later: REHEARSAL. Not due for two years."
45. **(Optional) the ring plate:** archaeology 2, "This ring answers the Hearts."
46. **Varro and the reversal:** "…then someone relit Kethra." She hands over the alloy. Card:
    "Level 3 complete. The Anchorage holds."
47. **Board the Wren.**

## Ending
48. **Repair station:** long-range comms repaired. Panel: "Send the ledger home?" (Transmit / Not
    yet).
49. **Ending cinematic:** the Wren is lit and alive, and rings of amber light leave the hull. Kethra
    glows green as the signal passes. Lines:
    - "Every name in the Anchorage ledger, and every log the Wren kept."
    - "Light carries them now, out past the Kessic Drift."
    - "Under Isilthe's sea, the Choir is still singing."
    - "This time, someone knows how to answer it."
50. **Credits:** "End of part one. The ledger goes home." A one-line recap, the team credit, every
    licensed asset, and then two buttons: Keep exploring / Title screen.

## Always available
- **Pause menu** (Esc): resume, settings, controls, restart this level, save and quit.
- **Character sheet** (Tab), **journal** (console on the Wren), **navigation map** (console on the
  Wren).
